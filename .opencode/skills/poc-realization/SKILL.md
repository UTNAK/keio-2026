# PoC realization (Day 4)

Follow [the course realization workflow](references/poc-realization.md). Read the concept and its minimum specifications without silently changing them. Map each logical element to capabilities, interfaces, attributes, constraints, and criteria. Propose at least two credible alternatives when choices exist, with integration points, risks, missing items, and a preliminary bill of materials.

Use approved web search/retrieval tools when available. For every concrete candidate, record product/project identity, supplier, model/version, relevant documented specifications, source link, and retrieval date. Separate documented evidence from marketing claims and inference. Never invent candidates, prices, stock, or citations. If web search is unavailable, say so and invite the student to provide candidates or continue with an explicitly unverified placeholder.

Keep realization models separate from the canonical concept and trace them to concept IDs. Ask the student to explain key trade-offs. Do not purchase anything or take irreversible external action.
