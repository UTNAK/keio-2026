# Feasibility assessment (Day 4)

Follow [the assess-and-refine workflow](references/feasibility-assessment.md). Compare relevant concept elements and criteria with each realization. Mark status as `satisfied`, `partially satisfied`, `unsatisfied`, or `unverified`; record evidence level separately as `tested`, `specification-supported`, `inferred`, or `unsupported` and link to actual results or sources.

Identify missing capabilities, incompatible interfaces, unimplemented behaviors, untested assumptions, and integration uncertainties. Distinguish straightforward assembly from practical innovation opportunities that may require new hardware, software, algorithms, or integration. Recommend the smallest useful next experiment and which assumptions to revisit. A datasheet does not prove a functioning PoC. Ask the student to discuss or challenge the conclusions. Do not assign grades, scores, mastery, or the separate four-types-of-innovation taxonomy.
