# Assess Feasibility -> Innovate and Refine

## Traceability matrix

| Criterion or concept ID | Candidate realization | Status | Evidence level | Evidence link/result | Gap or next question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `C-...` | `R-...` | satisfied / partially satisfied / unsatisfied / unverified | tested / specification-supported / inferred / unsupported | test, model check, or source | concrete unresolved issue |

Do not collapse status and evidence level. A criterion may appear satisfied according to a manufacturer specification while remaining untested in an integrated PoC. Explain the scope and date of each test/source, show incompatible or missing interfaces, and mark unsupported claims plainly.

For innovation opportunities, ask whether existing components can be combined directly or whether a new capability, algorithm, interface, or assembly is needed. Recommend a minimal experiment with an observable outcome. Invite the student to challenge assumptions before recording an iteration decision.
